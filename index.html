About Our Brand

Founded by architect Elena Vasilescu, Lumina emerged from a passion for blending architectural precision with the warmth of ambient lighting. Each piece in our collection is thoughtfully designed to transform spaces through the interplay of light and shadow.

Our lamps are more than just light sources; they are sculptural elements that enhance your living environment while providing functional illumination.
